About the collection

Same bird. Different interpretations.

These eight supplied animation files are presented individually, with their original filenames so each version is easy to identify. Open an animation for its full-page view and original controls.

Russian interface and story text has been translated into English. The animation artwork and behavior have been preserved. Bottom story captions and chapter lists are hidden; previews expand to show the full scene and playback controls.
